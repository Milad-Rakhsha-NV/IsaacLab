Material randomization and contact criteria

Each resetting environment samples its own density (1,400–1,800 kg/m³), internal friction (0.3–0.5), surface/internal friction ratio (0.6–1.0), strength multiplier (0.8–1.2), layer depth and commands. The material distribution widens after sustained episode completion and tracking performance.

Posture, joint limits, motion smoothness, foot slip, loaded/unloaded foot timing, non-foot contacts and foot placement use the rigid-control reward settings. RFT contact cues combine applied granular body impulses with rigid-floor collision contacts; granular loads are not discrete collision contacts.

The RFT force law includes an experimental near-rest bearing term. Finite-layer clipping does not model confinement, compaction or an evolving free surface. MPM is reserved for inference after training.
