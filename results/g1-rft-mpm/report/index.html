RFT randomization and curriculum settings

Each resetting environment receives an independent sample. Other environments retain their material. The curriculum starts with nominal material properties and 0–10 mm layers. The table shows target distributions at full difficulty.

ParameterTarget distribution
Layer depth over a fixed substrateUniform 0–118 mm
Bulk densityUniform 1,400–1,800 kg/m³

Surface/internal friction ratioUniform 0.6–1.0

Nominal values are 1,600 kg/m³, friction 0.4, interface ratio 1.0 and strength multiplier 1.0. Fixed RFT controls are bearing coefficient 0.87574, velocity regularization 0.04 m/s and zero inertial correction. Difficulty advances by 0.1 after two windows of at least 4,096 eligible episodes each, with at least 95% completion and at most 0.1 m/s mean tracking error. Before reaching full difficulty, a completion rate below 70% retreats one level and 10% of reset samples remain at the initial material range. Commands have no curriculum.

The 37-joint G1 uses its minimal asset with shared thigh/calf capsule colliders, plus its authored foot and torso colliders. Both policies use identical mechanics, actuators, observations, rewards and self-collision. RFT integrates loads over those collision surfaces.

Each panel compares 1.0 and 0.5 m/s using the same frozen checkpoint and seed 123. Sand starts 118 mm deep at density 1,600 kg/m³, internal friction 0.4 and surface/internal ratio 1.0. MPM uses PIC, a 20 mm grid, four substeps and the same prepared 12 × 3.2 m bed for both policies and speeds. The G1 MPM recordings use one coupling pass and feedback factor 1.0; the earlier Go2 main videos used 0.1. Controlled G1 tests compare both feedback factors and four-pass refinement.

MPM force convergence and material equivalence remain unvalidated. These are exploratory transfers. Support plots include contacts after failure, including non-foot contacts. A completed training budget is separate from passing gait checks.

Held-out validation measurements and criteria

These frozen-policy tests use no observation noise or action exploration. The randomized banks sample commands for both policies and full-range materials for RFT. Criteria require zero falls, valid foot placement in every environment, mean tracking error below 0.10 m/s and standing drift below 0.03 m/s. Loaded-foot velocity must stay below 0.10 m/s. Both feet must register at least three step events in each fixed walking trial. The loaded-foot metric uses ankle/body velocity and includes rolling; it is a proxy for slip.
